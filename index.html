# Voca Privacy Policy **Effective date: 6 July 2026** Voca ("the app") is an augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) app made to help non-speaking and minimally verbal children communicate. Privacy is a core design principle: **Voca does not collect, sell, or share any personal data. Ever.** ## What we collect Nothing. Voca has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no accounts. We (the developer) never receive, see, or store any information about you or your child. ## Where your data lives Everything you create in Voca — cards, photos, voice recordings, favourites, communication history and settings — is stored **on your device**. If you choose to turn on **Voca Family cloud sync**, your cards and settings are stored in your own **private iCloud database**, managed by Apple and protected by your Apple ID. This data is only accessible to you; the developer has no access to it. Sync is off by default and can be disabled at any time in Parent Settings. ## Device permissions Voca asks for permissions only when you use the matching feature, and the content stays on your device: - **Microphone** — to record your own voice for cards. - **Camera** — to take photos for cards. - **Photo library** — to choose photos for cards. ## Online image search When a parent uses the optional "Search Online" feature to find a card image, the search words you type are sent to **Pixabay** (pixabay.com), a free stock image service, in order to return results. No other information is sent, and this only happens when you actively search. Pixabay's privacy policy is available at https://pixabay.com/service/privacy/. Speech in Voca is generated entirely on your device using Apple's built-in voices. Nothing your child taps or says ever leaves the device. ## Children's privacy Voca is designed for use by children together with their parents, carers and therapists. Because the app collects no data at all, no personal information about any child is ever gathered, transmitted or stored by the developer. ## In-app purchases Voca offers optional "tip jar" purchases to support development. These are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive any payment details. ## Changes to this policy If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Since Voca collects nothing, any future change could only make this policy more specific, never less private. ## Contact Questions about privacy or the app: **jigsawappssupport@gmail.com**
