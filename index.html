Voca Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Voca is an augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) app made to help non-speaking and minimally verbal children communicate.

Voca has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no accounts. The developer never receives, sees or stores any information about you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no Voca server for your information to be sent to. We have no way to see what cards your child has, what they tap, what photos you add or what you record.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in Voca — cards, photos, voice recordings, favourites, day plans, communication history and settings — is stored on your device.

If you turn on iCloud sync, that information is also stored in your own private iCloud database, held by Apple under your Apple ID. The developer has no access to it. Apple does not grant app developers access to users' private or shared iCloud databases, and Voca does not use the public database that developers can read. Sync is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Parent Settings.

Family sharing

Voca can keep one child's board in step across several devices, including devices signed in to different Apple IDs — a parent's iPad, another parent's phone, a grandparent's tablet.

How it works, and what it means for privacy:

  • One person is the owner. The board lives in the owner's private iCloud database.
  • Other people join by invitation, using Apple's own sharing system. You send the invitation from the standard iOS share sheet, through whichever app you choose — Messages, Mail, or by copying a link. Voca never sends anything on your behalf.
  • People you invite can see the cards you have shared with them. A view-only set lets you share a hand-picked group of cards — for a grandparent, say — without giving access to the rest of the board.
  • You can see who has joined, and remove them, at any time.
  • The developer cannot see any of it. Shared boards live in Apple's shared iCloud database, which is as closed to us as the private one.

When you invite somebody, Apple shows you their name or contact details as part of its own sharing interface. That exchange is between you, them and Apple. It does not pass through us.

School Mode

School Mode is for schools, clinics and any setting where it must be demonstrable that nothing leaves the device.

With School Mode on, Voca makes no network connections of any kind. Everything your child creates or taps stays on that one device.

Voca has only four ways to reach the network, and School Mode closes all four:

  • iCloud sync and sharing — switched off, and any existing sync is stopped. Cards, photos and recordings are never uploaded.
  • Push notifications — Voca never registers with Apple's notification service, so no device token is ever issued.
  • Online picture search — switched off. Pictures can come only from the device's camera or photo library.
  • In-app purchases — switched off. No contact with the App Store while Voca is running.

These are enforced in the app's own networking code, not merely hidden from the settings screen, so no sequence of taps can cause a connection while School Mode is on. School Mode is set per device: turning it on for a school iPad has no effect on a family's own devices, and no information passes between them.

Speech

Speech is generated entirely on the device using Apple's built-in voices, or from voice recordings you make yourself. Nothing your child taps, and nothing Voca says, is ever sent anywhere.

Device permissions

Voca asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and the content stays on your device:

  • Microphone — to record your own voice for cards.
  • Camera — to take photos for cards.
  • Photo library — to choose photos for cards.

Push notifications

When iCloud sync is on, Voca registers with Apple's Push Notification service so that a device can be told when the shared board has changed. This is how a card added on one device appears on another.

These messages are silent and carry no content — they only signal that something changed, so the device knows to fetch it from your own iCloud database. Voca sends no notifications of its own, and the developer has no ability to send anything to your device. With School Mode on, or with sync off, Voca does not register at all.

Online image search

If a parent uses the optional Search Online feature to find a picture for a card, the words typed into that search box are sent to Pixabay, a free stock image service, so it can return results. Nothing else is sent, and only while you are actively searching. Pixabay's privacy policy is at pixabay.com/service/privacy.

This feature is unavailable in School Mode.

Children's privacy

Voca is made for children to use together with their parents, carers and therapists. Because the app gathers no data at all, no personal information about any child is ever collected, transmitted or stored by the developer. There is no sign-up, no profile and no way for a child to contact anyone through the app.

In-app purchases

Voca offers optional "tip jar" purchases to support development. These are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.
